## Scope

Homepage (`src/pages/index.astro`) and the shared shell (header, footer, page layout, mobile action bar) that every SBPS page inherits. Mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Audience and job

Prospective parents decide whether to enquire; current parents reach notices, fees and documents in one tap. Both carry equal weight. Proof: integrated NEET/JEE/NDA preparation on campus, and real sports results (38th National Games selections by sport, 2024 SFA runner-up). Only sourced facts; content run by school office staff.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is cut from the SBPS uniform. Woven house stripes in the crest's crimson, gold and green are the structure, not decoration. Refuses the Indian school default of ticker, banner slider and a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Blazer charcoal (#1d2226) type on cool shirting white; a deep crimson "tie" field for the homepage's lead panel and the footer; diagonal regimental stripes (gold thin, green broad) as the only ornament, used as section spines and tab edges; the crest as an embroidered patch. One sans family, Anek (Latin + Devanagari): condensed heavy widths for headlines and numerals, normal width for reading. Square corners (2px), 2px charcoal rules, no shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor learns that SBPS is a CBSE school (Nursery–XII) with competitive-exam preparation on campus and real sporting results. They believe it from the integrated-programme facts and the per-sport selection numbers. They act by requesting a call back, or a current parent jumps to notices, fees or documents.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop has two columns. Left (about 40%) is the crimson tie panel with a diagonal stripe edge. It holds the headline "School and NEET, JEE, NDA preparation under one roof" in condensed Anek at 64–72px, the line "CBSE · Nursery to XII · Dehradun · Learn to grow", and a working three-field call-back form whose gold button is the only gold action. Right is the real campus aerial photograph, full height. Two house-colour tabs (green "Admissions", charcoal "Current parents") sit along its foot, each with a one-line task list. On mobile the panel stacks first, the photo second with the tabs, and the action bar stays fixed.

FORM: The House Tie, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list (assigned by the roll). seed:456d3c123ff7b14d. Raises: lettered admission steps (sewing pattern envelope); gold reserved for one primary action (understory); one fixed reading measure, about 68ch (ice press); a stripe spine from the first viewport to the footer (Googie); tabular numerals in strict columns (datamatics); (teletext raise dropped after review: the school has not adopted notice reference numbers). Signature interaction: the tie stripe draws itself along the page spine as sections enter. It is static under reduced motion.
