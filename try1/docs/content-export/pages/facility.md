# Facility

Pickup and drop facility from various areas of the city is provided for the students. A fleet of buses is ready with highly trained drivers and conductors. Safety of children is the first and foremost priority of the drivers and conductors.

A healthy body and a healthy mind are a base for a progressing human being. So for this we have regular health check ups in school by renowned doctors from time to time.

Security system in the school is high tech. There are fire extinguishers, cctv cameras and guard at every gate. For the pre-primary, there are two teachers in each class with a maid. So the kids from all the classes are in safe hands and environment.
