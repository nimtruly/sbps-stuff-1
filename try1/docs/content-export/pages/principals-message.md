# Chairman (Dr. Baluni Group Of Institutions)

**
!
Dear all,Social Baluni Public School** is a unit of Mandakini Seva Samiti Education Society. Innovation, leadership and honesty are our core pillars that give Social Baluni School its unique character and identity.

Social Baluni Public School, the name is synonymous with excellence and sound deliverance; which is a result of immense hard work, dedication and the unquantified toil that goes into making each day count. Social Baluni Public School is an initiative to groom your child to become leader of tomorrow; for the new socio-economic order of the information age, who while maintaining the perspective of the world citizens will not lose their mooring in the rich cultural heritage that is every Indian’s pride.

Our efforts have always been directed towards the upliftment and betterment of our children; enabling them to progress and keep pace with the ever-changing and competitive world of today. We always believe that children of today are citizens of tomorrow and it is our duty to guide them towards their accomplishments and help them become independent and self-reliant.

We make best attempts to provide quality education to your child and render absolute support and assistance, to the best of our ability.

**We wish you a memorable school-life experience**.

**Dr.Navin BaluniM.B.B.S., M.D.**
