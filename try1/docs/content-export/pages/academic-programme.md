# Academic Programme

**Subjects taught in all classes are based on the CBSE guidelines.**

A balanced approach and combination of sports, music, dramatics,dance and computers with academics complies towards overall growth at Social Baluni Public School. At SBPS, classroom teaching will be interactive and will be appropriately supported by multimedia teaching aids, group discussions, seminars, visits by eminent guest faculty/speakers, field visits, project work etc. Literary, cultural, sports and information technology related activities form an integral part of learning at school. Comprehensive records will be maintained for all students to record their over all progress.

#### **Academic Session:**

The academic session is from April to March. It is divided into the following two terms.

**First term – April to SeptemberSecond Term – October to March**

Going to kindergarten is an important step for a child. It helps them learn skills that they will build on throughout their life.

At kindergarten your child will grow socially and emotionally. His/Her ability to think, use and recognize language and fine motor skills will be developed through play, art, dance, music, movement and interacting with others. Most importantly, your child will be learning to become an effective learner as he/she develops and extends the communication skills, build self-confidence, learn to be creative and develop skills that assist with reading, writing and mathematics.

The school is to be affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education, New Delhi and so it shall prepare its students for the All India Secondary School Examination and the All India Senior Certificate Examination (Board Exams for Classes X and XII respectively) conducted by the CBSE, New Delhi.
