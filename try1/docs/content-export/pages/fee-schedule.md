**General Rules :**

- Payment of school fees will be accepted by crossed cheque/demand draft/cash at City Office only. The MICR cheque/demand drafts may kindly be drawn in favour of “Social Baluni Public School, Dehradun” payable at Dehradun.

- The amount deposited towards registration and admission for a particular year is neither refundable nor transferable to any other year. Fresh generation will be required if the child is to be given admission for a subsequent admission test.

**Note :**

- The school reserves the right to revise the fee keeping in view the price index and other relevant consideration. However, such revision will not take place during the academic year barring exceptional circumstances.

- Participation for the Summer Camp will be optional and the change for different special activities during the camp will be communicated at a later stage.
