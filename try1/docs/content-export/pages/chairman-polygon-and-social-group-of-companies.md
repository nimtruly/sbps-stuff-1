# Chairman (Polygon and Social Group of Companies)

**
!
Social Baluni Public School** is one initiative in the state of Uttarakhand in the education sector. We have always shown dedication towards a brighter & better future of the children of Dehradun and the adjacent areas. There has been commendable contribution for the service of mankind by the Social group of companies in the state of Uttarakhand since last two decades, and as a part of our venture Social Baluni Public School is one such initiative. It is the school where we believe in adding value to the lives of our new generations by creating a connection with Indian traditions, culture and roots of the glorious India along with imparting knowledge of the New Era’s science and technology. Our emphasis is on moulding our future generation become good human beings who would serve the country and work with zeal for its prestige.

**Our aim is not just formal education, but creating awareness about the protection of environment, culture and our rich heritage. We provide quality education with minimum fees that would benefit Andrew suit every individual from different strata of the society.Dr. K.S. PanwarChairmanPolygon and Social Group of Companies**
