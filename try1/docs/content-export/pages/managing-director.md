# Managing Director (DBGI)

!

In the coming years 30th march 2014 shall be marked with golden letters in the history of Dehradun for establishing a school by the name of ‘Social Baluni Public School’ and make my words a living truth for the Education hub- Dehradun. 

The aim of the school is to motivate and promote each child to attain heights in the society. In the first year only the response we got was fabulous. There were around 400 students from different areas and sections of the city. With the motto of “learn to grow” we provide each child an opportunity to prove his/her potential and help in making learning easier through activity based teaching. I am confident of a better and positive response in the days ahead.
