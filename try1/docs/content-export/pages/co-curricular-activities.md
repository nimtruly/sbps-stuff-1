# Co-Curricular Activities

Social Baluni Public School believes in holistic education and does not focus merely on academic growth of it’s students. In an age where besides academic excellence other aspects for a harmonious personality are also treated very important, Baluni Public School, by incorporating several co-curricular activities into curriculum, strives to lend a cutting edge to the students and help them stand tall in the crowd. These activities are conducted through various Inter House activities at different levels and groups. A wide array of literary, cultural, sports and art related activities are conducted in a scheduled and well worked out manner which spreads over the entire academic year. The students are encouraged and guided to participate and win in these activities. The winners are awarded with Certificate of Merit. However, more than winning, participation is appreciated.

**Students will have a choice to be a part of one of the following clubs:**

- Shooting club

- Arts & Craft club

- Athletics club

- Archery club

- Fencing club

- Martial Arts club

- Cookery club

- Cricket club

- Photography club

- Football club

- Basketball club

- Yoga club

- Chess and Carrom club

- Table Tennis club
