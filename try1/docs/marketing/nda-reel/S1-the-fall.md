## Camera key

| Code | Camera and lens | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| A7S3 | Sony A7S III + 16–35mm f/2.8 | Hero shots, 4K 120p slow motion, low light, wide close-ups at 16mm |
| ZV | Sony ZV-E10 + 16–50mm f/3.5–5.6 | Second angle, dialogue, inserts and reactions at 50mm |
| OA4 | DJI Osmo Action 4 | Ground-level and chest-mount POV, mounted on obstacles, 4K 120p |
| 360 | Insta360 360° camera | Running follow shots on an invisible stick, wide reframes, 360° transitions |
| DRONE | Drone | Aerials, top-down formations, reveals, end-card plate |

Shot types: ECU (extreme close-up), CU (close-up), MCU (medium close-up), MS (medium), WS (wide), EWS (extreme wide), OTS (over the shoulder), POV (point of view), INS (insert).

## Shoot notes

- **Fall:** mat or soft ground, rehearsed controlled fall, first-aid kit and a teacher on set. For the V4 POV fall, the stunt cadet falls onto a mat with the OA4 on a chest mount. Lens protector on.
- **Slow motion:** A7S3 at 4K 120p, shutter 1/250, ND filter outdoors. OA4 at 4K 120p for ground-level shots.
- **360:** run behind or beside cadets on the invisible stick, then reframe to 9:16 in post.
- **Drone:** fly above students only with the school's permission and local rules. Keep 30m+ altitude over people, or film from the side.
- **Consent:** written parental consent for every student on camera.
- **Uniform:** PT kit and BDA bibs only. No army uniform or insignia on students.
- **Captions:** burn in Hinglish subtitles.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep text out of the bottom 20% and right edge.
