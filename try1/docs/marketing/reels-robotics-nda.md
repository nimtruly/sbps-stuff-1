### What the winners have in common

1. **A child is the first frame.** Not a building, not a banner, not a principal. A kid already mid-action.
2. **No intro voiceover.** Our 7K reel opens on "For the past two years, Social Baluni Public School has been nurturing..." None of the winners open on the school's name.
3. **The payoff is visual.** The robot works, the lights go off, everybody drops at once, the bar is cleared.
4. **Short beats the long ones on multiple.** 5s, 12s and 15s reels top the multiple chart. The long ones (SSB interview, hydrocar) win because there is a human conversation to follow.
5. **Comment fights drive shares.** "Girls vs Boys" and "can you name them" make people tag friends.
6. **Phone footage is fine.** The 15.8M hydrocar and 5.17M interview were shot on a phone in an ordinary room.

### Rejected (do not copy)

- **AI-generated robot videos** (@electrolab_latur seed planting robot, likely @roboblockiq river bot). They look real but are rendered. Copying the look would be a false claim for a school.
- **Army motivation edits** (@olivepaltan_official, @defence_lovers121, @shaurya_negitkd). They use NDA/IMA footage the school does not own.
- **Coaching-ad reels with teachers lecturing** (@khungaacademy). Views come from parents searching syllabus, not from kids.

## 5. Production rules (all 10)

- **Consent:** written parental consent for every child on camera. Keep the forms.
- **First frame:** a child mid-action. Never the school building, logo or principal.
- **No intro voiceover.** The school name goes in the last 3 seconds and in the caption, not the opening.
- **Text:** 6 words or fewer per card. Keep inside the safe zone (nothing in the top 230px, the bottom 480px or the right 230px of a 1080x1920 frame). Burn in captions.
- **Real lines only.** Where the script says "TO CONFIRM" or "own words", let the kid or teacher say it their way. A scripted kid sounds scripted.
- **Shoot vertical, 4K if possible**, ordinary daylight. Phone is fine; every top reel here was phone-shot.
- **Music:** Instagram library only, so the boost isn't blocked.
