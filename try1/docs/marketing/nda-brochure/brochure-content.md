**Salient Features:**

- **Written Coaching:** The Academy conducts written coaching programme for various entries like NDA, CDS AFCAT, ACC, RIMC, RMS, SS and SSB etc.
- **Personal Attention:** One of the key feature for training at the BDA is that, we provide personalised guidance to each of our candidates. By virtue of this the classes are made more interactive, interesting and meaningful to the candidates.
- **Experienced Faculty:** The Academy has a dedicated set of professional, competitive & seasoned faculty for all the subjects i.e. Maths / English / General Studies / General Ability Practice, science and humanities. The faculty members are fully devoted in imparting result oriented knowledge & education.
- **Mock Test:** The nitty-gritties related to cracking the competitive written examination are taken care off, by conducting tests on routine basis i.e. weekly / monthly. Mock tests are conducted to monitor and evaluate the performance/progress of each candidate and due rewards maintained. Once the correction of answer-sheet has been done, one on one individual counseling of each candidate/student is carried out. After the candidates have been apprised of their performance, all possible corrective measures to be undertaken by each students are suggested.
- **Time Management:** It is a very well established fact that time is always at premium, while attempting any competitive examination. Therefore in order to succeed, time management technique/tips to successfully complete the paper in a given time frame is also taught to the candidate. Extensive practice in order to master the technique is imparted to the candidates, by administering them with variety of mock test on regular basis.

## 6. Things to confirm before using in public content

These are inconsistencies or gaps in the brochure itself. Do not put them on screen or in captions until the school confirms them.

1. **Starting class.** The brochure gives three different ones: "Class 5 onwards" (flyer and cover, Foundation V–XII), "from Class VIII for 10 years" (Hindi section) and "right from Class IX" (chairman).
2. **"Students selected in NDA".** Only 5 of the 20 names say "SSB Clear". Find out whether the other 15 cleared only the written exam or reached the final merit list.
3. **Rupesh Kumar** is listed twice. Are these two people, or a duplicate?
4. **"ACL" and "RIMS"** on the cover are probably misprints for ACC and RIMC, because the other pages say ACC and RIMC.
5. **Defence Academy vs BDA.** The brochure uses "Doon Baluni Defence Academy" and "BDA" for the same academy. Choose one name for the reel captions.
6. **NCC, gym, rooms, bedding and mess** are not shown anywhere in the brochure. Before filming, confirm they exist and that we have permission to shoot them.
7. **Cover imagery.** The Army, Navy and Air Force photos on the cover look like stock or official photos. Do not reuse them in reels.
8. **Minors' names and photos.** Results pages show students' names and faces. Get consent before featuring any of them in a reel.
