# N3 · "Subah 5 Baje" (5 AM)

**Topic:** NDA · **Runtime:** 25s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps
**Reference:** [A1 @mihir_photoart_17 "Time Travel", 2.82M, 8,787x](REFERENCES.md) (empty spaces + remembered voices), applied to the parade ground

## Logline
The school parade ground before dawn, empty and in fog. Commands echo with nobody there: "Savdhaan!", "Vishraam!", "DauD ke!". Then one pair of boots steps into the frame, then ten, then all of them.

## Why it can travel
The empty-space technique is the highest-multiple cinematic format we found. On a foggy Doon morning, a ground that "remembers" drill is eerie and beautiful, and the reveal (bodies filling the empty frame) is a strong visual payoff.

## Look
- **Camera:** locked frames. Stillness is the point. One slow push at the end.
- **Lens:** 24mm for empty wides; 100mm for details (whistle on a hook, dew on the flag rope).
- **Depth of field:** deep for the empty wides (so the emptiness reads), very shallow for details.
- **Light:** blue hour into first light. Fog (natural winter fog, or a low-lying fogger). The first sunlight should hit exactly as the contingent fills the ground.
- **Grade:** cold blue to warm gold over 25 seconds.
- **Sound:** wild recordings of real drill commands, treated with echo and distance, as if the ground remembers them. Real boots at the end, dry and close.
