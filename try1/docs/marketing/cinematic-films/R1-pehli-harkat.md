# R1 · "Pehli Harkat" (The First Move)

**Topic:** Robotics · **Runtime:** 45s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps with 48/96fps inserts
**Reference:** [A9 @jeskoo_0 robot arm, 7.85M, 17,785 shares](REFERENCES.md) (process montage to first move)

## Logline
Three weeks of a student's robot in 45 seconds: pencil, paper, wires, failure, failure, and then the arm moves for the first time. The whole film earns one moment.

## Why it can travel
The reference got 17,785 shares, the highest in the robotics set, on nothing but hands, paper and one payoff. People share the moment something comes alive. We add faces, a school, and a Hinglish ending.

## Look
- **Lens:** 100mm macro for hands and wires; 35mm for the room; 85mm f/1.4 for faces.
- **Depth of field:** very shallow throughout. Faces always against blurred lab practicals (LED strips, monitor glow) so the background turns to bokeh.
- **Light:** night interior. Monitor and desk lamp as the only key; cool blue fill from the window. Warm tungsten practical on the robot so it reads "alive" against the cold room.
- **Grade:** teal-shadow, warm-highlight. Grain on.
- **Sound:** no music for the first 30s. Pencil scratch, keyboard, servo whine, a sigh. Music drops in on the first move.
