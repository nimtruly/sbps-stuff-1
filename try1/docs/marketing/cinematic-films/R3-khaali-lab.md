# R3 · "Khaali Lab" (The Empty Lab)

**Topic:** Robotics · **Runtime:** 25s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps
**Reference:** [A1 @mihir_photoart_17 "Time Travel", 2.82M on 321 followers, 8,787x, 11,653 shares](REFERENCES.md) (empty spaces + remembered voices)

## Logline
The AI & Robotics Lab at night, completely empty. As the camera drifts through it, we hear the day that happened here: kids arguing, a robot crashing, a teacher, a cheer. At the end, one robot on a desk still has its light on.

## Why it can travel
The reference is the highest-multiple cinematic reel we found. It has no people, only rooms and remembered lines, and the viewer fills it with their own school days. We use the same device for a lab full of tomorrow's work, which is new on Instagram.

## Look
- **Camera:** gimbal or slider, one continuous-feeling drift (3-4 hidden cuts through dark objects).
- **Lens:** 24mm for space, 50mm for details.
- **Depth of field:** medium-shallow (f/2.8). Foreground objects (a half-built robot, a 3D printer nozzle, a LEGO wall) pass soft in front of the lens as wipes.
- **Light:** only practicals. Monitor standby LEDs, a 3D printer's light, exit sign, moonlight through glass. Each voice line is "lit" by one practical flickering on.
- **Grade:** teal night, one warm source at the end.
- **Sound:** the whole film is the soundtrack of the day, recorded on location during a real class (wild audio). Echo-treated, like memory.
