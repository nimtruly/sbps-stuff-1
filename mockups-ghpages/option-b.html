How admission works

Registration runs from the last week of December to the last week of February.

1Register

Fill in the form from the registration booklet at the school office.

2Admission test

You receive a letter with your roll number, date and time.

3Interview

Students who qualify in the test are called for an interview.

4Admission

Provisional until documents are submitted and the fee is paid.
