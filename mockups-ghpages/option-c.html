How admission works

Registration runs from the last week of December to the last week of February.

Apply onlineFees

1
Register

Fill in the form from the registration booklet and hand it to the school office.

2
Admission test

The office gives you a letter with your registration number, roll number, date and time of the test.

3
Interview

Students who qualify in the test are called for an interview.

4
Admission

Depends on the test, the interview and the seats available. Provisional until documents are submitted and the fee is paid.
