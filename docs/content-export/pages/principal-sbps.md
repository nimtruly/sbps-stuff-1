# Principal (SBPS)

!

“**A dream written down with a date becomes a goal. A goal broken down into steps becomes a plan. A plan backed by action makes your dreams come true”- Greg Reid**

At SBPS , we leave no stone unturned to offer multitudes of opportunities to our children; it is for them to make the most of it. Children are motivated to grab every opportunity that comes their way which helps them in their holistic growth and strengthen their belief in teamwork, which is important in this fast paced world. They understand the importance of emotional balance, critical thinking and most importantly accepting failure graciously. We strive to endow them with all these qualities and many more, We believe in empowering children in such a manner that they act as representatives of a meaningful and value based society. Our pedagogy is holistic and comprehensive and compliments in this direction. We are a team of fabulous faculty members who display boundless energy and intense commitment which keeps the ethos of our school shining brightly.

**Mr. Pankaj NautiyalM.Sc., B.Ed. M.A, LLBPrincipal**
