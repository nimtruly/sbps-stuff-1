# SBPS Day Boarding

**SBPS provides DAY BOARDING, an appropriate and safe environment for the holistic development of students while granting peace to the parent’s minds. Admitting their ward to the day boarding school makes parents feel that their children are involved in productive activities and safe hands.**

Working parents don’t get enough time to share with their children. Parents feel insecure about their children are left out alone for long hours. Choosing a day boarding school is the best option as it offers all the arrangements from effective learning to extra co-curricular activities in a healthy environment.

Day Boarding allows children spend most of their time interacting with the teachers even more than their parents. This creates a unique and stronger bond with the teacher and due to the small class size, they get personalised mentorship, leading to the holistic development of children.

The overall cost of studying in day boarding schools is less expensive than boarding schools.The school provides day meals, health care, and sports facilities while maintaining safety and security.

Children are potentially influenced by their parents. Living with their parents, a child feels loved, cared and motivated.Day Boarding permits parents to take their child home after day. After returning home a child gets necessary parental monitoring and quality time spend during festivals & other family functions.

**Cost-Effective. **It is quite affordable as there will be no extra cost of living, food, transportation. It also Saves the parents from botheration & inconvenience.
