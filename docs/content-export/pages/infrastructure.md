# Infrastructure

#### Administrative Block

The administrative block is the backbone of the school. A fully computerized fee and management office, stationery store etc, are the other highlights. There are audio visual facilities for seminars, workshops and meetings for the training of the teaching and the administrative staff. A spacious lobby for parents and visitors is an important part of the administrative block.

#### **Academic Block**

The magnificent academic building is for all the classes. Split level planning ensures ample light and proper ventilation to enhance the comfort level. In addition to classrooms, the building also has a library computer laboratory, science laboratories, music room, dance room, drama room, teacher resource room and much more. The higher preparatory section has a separate library and computer room for the toddlers. A separate play area is also restricted for the exclusive use of the kindergarten children.

#### **Classrooms**

The airy and spacious classrooms are well designed, subject oriented with plenty of natural light and ventilation that can comfortably accommodate 50 students with ample working space.

#### **Computer Centre**

Small opportunities are often the beginning of great achievements. Knowledge of computers is instilled in the students right from the primary section. The separate computer lab for primary and secondary students is equipped with modern and latest hardware and software. A well trained teaching staff as well as technical staff provides ample guidance and caters to the needs of the students.

#### **Library**

An ultra modern computerized library acts as a resource centre accommodating carefully selected volumes at the disposal of the students as well as the staff. Besides being a very comfortable and ideal platform for self study, it also houses a treasure of resource materials like journals, magazines, computer software etc.

#### **Audiovisual Laboratory**

Spacious audiovisual laboratories with a capacity to accommodate over 200 students, is utilized for multimedia presentations also. There is an excellent collection of films and documentaries based on education as well as entertainment. This laboratory facilitates interactive learning.

#### **Laboratories**

Well equipped laboratories for different streams of science with excellent facilities for practical work, complement the theoretical knowledge.

#### **Language Laboratory**

Language means communication. Learning of language is an ongoing process. The approach now a days is on scientific and communicative methods. Acquiring linguistic perfection is the basic aim of the language laboratory.

#### **Multi Purpose Hall**

A multipurpose hall is there for indoor games facilities like Badminton, Taekwondo, shooting, Table Tennis and other indoor activities.

#### **Staff Room**

Separate staff rooms are provided for the teaching staff. In addition, adequate washroom facilities and drinking water facilities are provided at convenient places in the school building.

#### **Art Room**

Practicing art brings peace and a sense of happiness to the mind. A separate art studio our caters the need of different types of art forms like basic drawing, sketching and painting to professional art work.

#### **Music Room**

“Music gives the soul to the universe, wings to the mind, flight to the imagination and life to everything”

As quoted by Plato we also believe in this thought and thus provide a wide spectrum of opportunities for students in the field of music.
