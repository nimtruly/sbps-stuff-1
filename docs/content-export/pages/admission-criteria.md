# Admission criteria

Registration for admission begins from last week of December to last week of February. For registration every candidate will be required to fill up a registration form attached with a booklet and the same will be submitted to the office in charge of the school.

The office in-charge issues a letter to each candidate stating registration number, roll number, date and time, etc. for the admission test when he/she submits the duly filled in registration form.

Admission is granted based on the child’s performance in the test and availability of seats in the school. Qualified candidates will be called for the interview. Based on the performance of both the written test as well as interview, the candidate is granted admission. All admissions are provisional until required documents are submitted and fee is deposited.

Social Baluni Public School commenced its first session in 2014-2015 from classes Nursery to VIII. In session 2015 the school started class IX and the same students will appeared for class XII CBSE exam in 2018.
