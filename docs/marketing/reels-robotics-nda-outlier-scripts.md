# SBPS reels: robotics and NDA

Research date: 16 Nov 2026. Account: [@sbpsdehradun](https://www.instagram.com/sbpsdehradun/) (also cross-posted to Facebook).
Goal: organic views first, then boost the reels that take off. Language: Hinglish. Students act and speak (parental consent forms needed).

## 1. Where the account stands

- 1,308 followers, 245 posts.
- Last 15 reels: median about 8,500 views, best 28,100, worst 3,200.
- That is already about 6x followers per reel, so reels do reach non-followers. The gap is the format, not the reach.
- Not audited: watch time, hold at 3 seconds, shares. Those need Insights screenshots from the account owner.

## 2. What the research covered

- 22 Instagram keyword searches (robotics, school science projects, NDA, Sainik school, NCC, parades, school kids).
- 1,035 reels catalogued, view counts pulled for the 516 with 1,500+ likes.
- 24 reels downloaded. I looked at frame sheets for all 24. Audio transcription (local Whisper, small model) was only reliable on the talking-head reels; most of the others are music beds with no speech, which the frames confirm.
- Follower counts checked for 9 reference accounts, so the "views vs followers" multiples below are real. Others are ranked on views alone.

## 3. What is working

### Robotics and science

| Pattern | Evidence | Why it travels |
|---|---|---|
| One student, one working model, payoff on camera | D.N. Public School girl purifies muddy water: 7.4M on a school account (27x followers). Student's clothes-folding machine: 35.9M on a 6.8K account. "Student made a road that generates electricity": 44M | The viewer waits to see if it really works. The model is in frame from second zero. |
| A challenge other kids try and fail | "Try to steal the diamond" (alarm box, kids take turns): 11.3M on a 6.8K account | Game format. Each attempt is a new mini-payoff, so people stay. |
| Named students, named class, teacher at the end | RBPS "Project Hydrocar", four Class 9 boys: 15.8M on a 13.9K account | Looks real, not staged. Parents share it. |
| Teacher + small kids' genuine reactions | Dronacharya Public School "ultrasound prank to avoid junk food": 166M on an 11K account | Kids' faces are the content. |

What does **not** work: lab tours and "our state-of-the-art STEM lab" montages. Every one I found sat between 4K and 20K views. That is the format most schools post, and it is the one to avoid.

## 5. Rules for every script below

- Anything in `{{double braces}}` is a fact the school must fill in. Nothing is invented.
- No greeting, no logo sting, no "welcome to SBPS". The video starts mid-action.
- On-screen text: six words or fewer, up from frame 1, kept out of the bottom 20% and top 10%.
- Burn in captions. Record real sound (buzzer, motors, boots, commands).
- One ask at the end. No phone number in the video; the admissions line goes in the caption and in the boosted version.
- Children say their lines in their own words. Give them the idea, not a script to recite. Stiff recitation is what kills school reels.

## 6. Robotics scripts

### R1. "Chura ke dikhao" (the challenge)

Reference: Try to steal the diamond, 11.3M. Length: 25-30s.
Needs: a box with a sensor and buzzer built by students (IR or ultrasonic sensor + buzzer; confirm the kit has these), one chocolate bar.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Close-up: a hand creeps toward a chocolate in a box. Buzzer screams. Hand jerks back. | (buzzer) | Chura ke dikhao 🍫 |
| 2-5s | Builder student, grinning, arms folded. | "Yeh chocolate aaj tak koi nahi chura paaya." | |
| 5-16s | Four quick attempts, 2-3s each: slow hand, fast grab, from behind, with a ruler. Buzzer every time. Real laughter. | (ad-lib reactions) | Attempt 1 ❌ ... 4 ❌ |
| 16-21s | A teacher walks up, confident. Tries. Buzzer. Class erupts. | Teacher: "Arre!" | Ma'am bhi ❌ |
| 21-27s | Builder flips the box, points at the sensor. | "Andar ek sensor hai. Haath paas aate hi pakad leta hai. Humne banaya hai, Class {{class}} mein." | Class {{class}} ne banaya |
| 27-30s | Builder eats the chocolate. | "Chocolate meri." | |

Caption: `Is chocolate ko churana namumkin hai. Aap hote toh kaise try karte? 👇` then school name and admissions line.
Ask: "Comment mein batao, aap kaise churate?"

### R2. "Giregi ya rukegi?" (the suspense)

Reference: clothes-folding machine and footstep-power reels (payoff you wait for). Length: 15-20s.
Needs: an obstacle-avoiding or edge-detecting robot car. If the kit car cannot detect a table edge, run it at a wall of stacked notebooks or a glass of water instead.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Low angle: robot car driving straight at the table edge (or the glass). | Student off-camera: "Giregi ya rukegi?" | Giregi ya rukegi? |
| 2-8s | Cut to three kids' faces watching, wincing. Back to the car, closer. Slow motion for the last half second. | (gasps) | |
| 8-11s | Car stops dead at the edge. Kids cheer. | "Ruk gayi!" | Ruk gayi ✅ |
| 11-17s | Builder picks it up, shows the sensor to camera. | "Yeh sensor aage ka raasta dekhta hai. Code humne khud likha hai." | Code: Class {{class}} |
| 17-20s | Car set down again, heading for the edge. Cut before the result. | "Ek baar aur?" | |

The cut before the second result is the loop: the replay starts on the same question.
Caption: `Aapko kya laga, giregi? Sach batana.`
Ask: one word in comments, "Giregi" or "Rukegi".

### R3. "Bina haath lagaye" (one student, one take)

Reference: D.N. Public School water model, 7.4M. Length: 20-25s.
Needs: one confident student from a junior class and any working sensor project (smart dustbin, automatic light, door alarm). Younger is better here.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Student already mid-action, project on the desk in front, school building behind. | "Bina haath lagaye yeh dustbin khol ke dikhaun?" | Bina chhue khulega? |
| 3-8s | Waves hand. Lid opens. Does it twice. | "Dekho. Ek baar aur." | |
| 8-18s | Camera pushes in as the student points at parts. | "Yahan sensor hai. Haath paas aaya, isne motor ko bola, motor ne dhakkan khola." | Sensor → Motor → Dhakkan |
| 18-23s | Back to the student's face. | "Mera naam {{first name}} hai, main Class {{class}} mein hoon, aur yeh maine banaya hai." | {{first name}}, Class {{class}} |

Shoot it in one take, outdoors, daylight. Do not cut away to the lab.
Caption: `{{first name}}, Class {{class}}. Dustbin ko haath lagana mana hai.`
Ask: "Agla project kaun sa dekhna hai? Comment karo."

### R4. "9 baar fail" (the failures)

Reference: REELS SONNET idea, backed by the "wait for the end" reels. Length: 25-30s.
Needs: real footage of the robot failing. Film every attempt during a normal robotics period. Use the true count.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Robot drives off the track and falls over. | Student groans. | {{N}} baar FAIL |
| 2-12s | Fast cuts of real failures: wheel comes off, wrong turn, nothing happens, wire pulled. Counter ticks up. | (real reactions only) | Fail 1, 2, 3 ... |
| 12-16s | Two students bent over the laptop, arguing about the code. | "Left sensor ulta laga hai!" (whatever they really say) | |
| 16-24s | The run that works, start to finish, no cuts. | Silence, then cheering. | Attempt {{N+1}} |
| 24-28s | Student to camera, out of breath. | "{{N}} baar fail hua. Tab jaake chala." | |

Caption: `Robotics mein sabse pehle fail hona sikhate hain.`
Ask: "Save karo, agli baar jab kuch na chale tab dekhna."
Note: if the true number of failures is small, say the true number. "3 baar fail" still works.

### R5. "Ek cadet insaan nahi hai" (robotics meets NDA)

No direct reference; it joins the two strongest patterns (sync marching + a working student robot). Length: 15-20s. This is the one only SBPS can make, because it needs both programmes in one frame.
Needs: a line-following or remote-driven robot, a squad of NDA-wing students in drill order.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Low shot of boots marching in step. Camera pans down the line. | Drill command, real audio. | Ek cadet insaan nahi hai |
| 3-8s | Last in line: a small robot rolling in step behind the squad. | (boots) | 👀 |
| 8-12s | "Squad, tham!" Everyone halts. The robot halts a beat late. | Command audio. | |
| 12-17s | Cadets hold straight faces. One cracks a smile. The builder runs in and salutes the robot. | "Class {{class}} ka banaya hua cadet." | Banaya: Class {{class}} |

Caption: `Hamari sabse chhoti recruit. Naam suggest karo.`
Ask: "Iska naam kya rakhein? Comment karo." (name suggestions are easy comments)

## 7. NDA scripts

### N1. "Normal school vs hamara school"

Reference: school bus comparison, 2.4M from 183 followers. Length: 20-25s.
Needs: the same students playing both sides. Only show things that really happen at SBPS; swap out any pair that is not true.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4s | Split or hard cut. Left: a class drones it out, slouching. Right: NDA wing snaps to attention. | "Good moooorning ma'am." / "Jai Hind, sir!" | Normal school vs Hamara school |
| 4-9s | Bell rings: kids stroll out chatting. / Bell rings: they fall in, in threes. | (bell) | Chhutti ki bell |
| 9-14s | Late-comer: "Sorry ma'am, bus late thi." / Late-comer drops and starts push-ups without being told. | as shown | Late aaye toh |
| 14-19s | PT period: one kid hides behind a tree. / Squad running in step, counting aloud. | "Ek! Do! Teen!" | PT period |
| 19-23s | Both versions of the same boy, side by side. He looks at the other one. | "Yeh bhi main hi hoon." | NDA prep, Class 9 se |

"NDA prep, Class 9 se" is the only programme claim in the reel, and it is the one the school's own documents support.
Caption: `Aap kaun se wale the? 😄`
Ask: "Tag karo us dost ko jo hamesha late aata tha."

### N2. "Class 9. NDA interview."

Reference: 11-year-old's interview, 14M at 4 minutes; live NDA interview, 3.2M. Length: 40-45s for the reel, with a longer cut saved for later.
Needs: one student who speaks well and the NDA instructor. Ask real questions and keep the real answers. Do not write the answers.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Student knocks, enters, stands at attention at the chair. | "May I come in, sir?" | Class 9. NDA interview. |
| 3-8s | Instructor, not looking up. | "Baitho. Fauj mein kyun jaana hai? Salary ke liye?" | Pehla sawaal |
| 8-20s | Tight on the student's face. Let the pause run. | (the student's real answer) | (captions) |
| 20-32s | Instructor pushes back. | "Agar selection nahi hua toh?" | Doosra sawaal |
| 32-40s | Student's answer. Instructor nods slightly. | (real answer) | |
| 40-44s | Student stands, salutes, turns smartly. | "Jai Hind, sir." | Aap kya jawab dete? |

Shoot two cameras if possible (one on each face), or shoot the instructor's questions again afterwards for cutaways.
Caption: `"Agar selection nahi hua toh?" Aap kya jawab dete?`
Ask: answer the question in comments.

### N3. "Fauji"

Reference: "फौजी", 13M on a 5.8K school account. Length: 15s.
Needs: a full classroom and one student who genuinely wants to join the forces. The line has to be true for the child who says it.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Teacher's point of view, whole class seated. | Teacher: "Bade hoke kya banoge?" | Bade hoke kya banoge? |
| 3-8s | Quick answers from seats. | "Doctor." "Engineer." "YouTuber." (laughter) | |
| 8-11s | One student stands up, straight. Room goes quiet. | "Fauji, ma'am." | फौजी |
| 11-15s | Hard cut: same student at dawn drill, in step with the squad. Hold on the face. Fade to black and white. | (boots, one command) | Taiyari Class 9 se |

Caption: `Kuch bachche jawab sochte nahi. 🇮🇳`
Ask: none. This one is built to be shared, and an ask would cheapen it.

### N4. "Aise chalte hain" (7-second loop)

Reference: NCC walk reel, 3.1M at 7 seconds. Length: 7-9s.
Needs: 6-8 students in drill boots, a phone held low.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Top-down shot: boots lined up toe to toe, still. | "Squad!" | Normal walk ❌ |
| 2-7s | "Tez chal!" All step off together, camera follows the feet. Heel strikes land on the beat. | (boots only) | NDA wing walk ✅ |
| 7-8s | Halt. Feet snap together, same framing as the first shot. | "Tham!" | |

The last frame matches the first, so it loops. Post it exactly this short.
Caption: `Awaaz on karke dekho. 🔊`
Ask: none.

### N5. "Rank pehchano"

Reference: Army legends poster, 3.9M on 337 followers (save-able reference), crossed with a quiz so children are on camera. Length: 25-30s.
Needs: printed rank insignia cards (check each against the official Indian Army rank chart before shooting), 4-5 students.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | A card is held up to camera, a student squints at it. | Instructor: "Yeh kaun si rank hai? 5 second." | Rank pehchano. 5 second. |
| 3-8s | On-screen countdown. Student answers. Reveal. | "Captain!" / "Sahi." | 5...4...3...2...1 |
| 8-20s | Three more cards, faster each time. Keep one honest wrong answer and the laugh that follows. | (answers) | Score: 1/1, 2/2, 2/3 ... |
| 20-25s | Hardest card. The student hesitates. Cut before the answer. | "Yeh wali... " | Aapko pata hai? |
| 25-30s | Full chart of all the ranks on one clean card. | "Jawab comments mein." | Save kar lo |

Caption: `Aakhri wali rank kaun si hai? Bina Google kiye batao.`
Ask: answer in comments. The chart at the end is what gets saved.

## 8. Shoot order and posting

Two shoot days cover all ten.

- **Day 1, robotics period:** R4 first (film every real attempt), then R1, R2, R3.
- **Day 2, morning drill:** N4, N3's drill shot, R5, N1's "hamara school" halves. Then classroom: N3, N1's "normal" halves, N2, N5.

Posting, three reels a week for about three and a half weeks, alternating topics:

| Week | Tue | Thu | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | N4 (7s loop) | R1 (challenge) | N3 (Fauji) |
| 2 | R2 (suspense) | N1 (comparison) | R3 (one take) |
| 3 | N2 (interview) | R5 (robot cadet) | N5 (rank quiz) |
| 4 | R4 (9 baar fail) | boost the top two | |

Post around 7-8 pm. The hour matters far less than the first two seconds.
Boost rule: any reel that passes 3x the account's median (about 25,000 views) in 72 hours gets ad money. For the boosted copy, add the admissions line and phone number to the caption, not to the video.

## 9. Needed from the school before shooting

1. Class numbers and first names for every `{{ }}` blank.
2. Which sensor projects actually work today (R1 needs a proximity alarm, R2 an obstacle or edge sensor, R3 any hands-free project, R5 a line follower or remote car).
3. Signed parental consent for every child whose face appears.
4. Confirmation that every "hamara school" moment in N1 really happens at SBPS.
5. Whether Doon Baluni Defence Academy (@doon_balunidefenceacademy) is the school's sister institution. If so, cross-post the NDA reels there and tag both accounts.
6. Insights screenshots for the last 10 reels (reach, watch time, shares) so the next plan uses the account's own numbers.

## 10. Limits of this research

- Instagram search is personalised, so this is a strong sample, not a census.
- Follower multiples are verified for 9 accounts only.
- The hook-scoring tool in the reel skill is built for English and could not grade Hinglish lines (it returned its floor score for most), so the hooks here are chosen on the reference reels' evidence, not on that score.
- Audio transcription was unreliable on reels with music beds; the analysis leans on the frames for those.
