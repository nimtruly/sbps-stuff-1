# N5 · "Unki Virasat" (Their Legacy)

**Topic:** NDA · **Runtime:** 30s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps
**Reference:** [A11 @doon_balunidefenceacademy "Army Legends", 3.87M on 330 followers, 11,713x](REFERENCES.md) (our own sister academy; the subject is proven on our exact audience)

## Logline
A dark classroom. A projector throws the face of an Uttarakhand army legend onto the wall. A student in uniform steps into the beam, and the portrait falls across his face. He says the name and regiment. Then the next legend, the next student. Eight legends, eight students, one beam of light.

## Why it can travel
Our sister academy got 3.87M views with a static poster of these same faces. This turns the poster into a film: the faces of today's students literally carry the faces of the legends. It is visual, local, emotional, and it ends on a loop.

## Look
- **Light:** a single projector beam (or a large LED panel with a gobo of the portrait) as the only key. Haze in the room so the beam is visible. Everything else is black.
- **Lens:** 85mm and 50mm. Faces close.
- **Depth of field:** f/1.4-2. The student's eyes sharp; the portrait projected on his face partly soft; the rest of the class rows lost in the dark.
- **Movement:** slow push-ins; one lateral slider move through the beam for transitions (each new student steps into the beam as the camera passes).
- **Grade:** deep blacks, warm projector colour, slight red in the shadows.
- **Sound:** projector fan, a single sustained note. Each name spoken softly, close-mic'd. Drum on the final salute.

## Legends
Use only Uttarakhand-linked names and verify every rank, unit and spelling against an official source (Indian Army, regimental history) before shooting. The sister academy's poster used names including Rfn Jaswant Singh Rawat and Gen Bipin Rawat; confirm each one for this film rather than copying the poster.
