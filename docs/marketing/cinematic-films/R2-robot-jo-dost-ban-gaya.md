# R2 · "Chhotu" (The Robot That Became a Friend)

**Topic:** Robotics · **Runtime:** 30s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps
**Reference:** [A7 @nikolay_tyulkin "Watti" robotic lamp, 1.34M, 283x](REFERENCES.md) (a robot with personality)

## Logline
A small student-built robot is left alone in a classroom after school. It "wakes up", explores the desks, finds a Class 3 kid who forgot her bag, and the two of them play. Shot like an animated short, from the robot's height.

## Why it can travel
The reference worked because the machine had a character, not a spec sheet. A child plus a robot with a personality is the "intrigue till the end" hook: what is it going to do next?

## Look
- **Camera height:** 15-25 cm off the floor for the whole film. The world is giant.
- **Lens:** 24mm and 35mm at floor level for scale; 85mm for the robot's "face".
- **Depth of field:** shallow, f/1.8-2.8. Desk legs and chairs become soft foreground pillars the camera slides between.
- **Light:** late-afternoon sun through classroom windows, long shadows, dust in the beams (haze machine, light).
- **Grade:** warm, soft contrast, like an animated film.
- **Sound:** designed robot "voice" from servo sounds and beeps (no words), small playful score.
