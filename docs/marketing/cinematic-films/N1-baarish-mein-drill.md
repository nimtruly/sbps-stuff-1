# N1 · "Baarish Mein Drill" (Drill in the Rain)

**Topic:** NDA · **Runtime:** 25s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps + 96fps
**References:** [A4 @cadetunplugged20, 1.97M, 1,348x](REFERENCES.md) and [A5 @kr_praveen27, 2.62M, 889x](REFERENCES.md) (same image, hit twice: a lone cadet marching in rain)

## Logline
Monsoon morning, empty school ground. Everyone else has run for cover. One Class IX NDA aspirant keeps marching, alone, in the rain. A letter-style voiceover to his future self.

## Why it can travel
Two separate accounts got 2M+ views with the same image: one figure, a wet parade ground, unbroken discipline. It is the most proven cinematic NDA image we found. We make it ours with a 14-year-old, our ground and our words instead of the borrowed breakup audio.

## Look
- **Lens:** 200mm telephoto from far away for the main wide, compressing rain streaks and the school building behind; 85mm for close details.
- **Depth of field:** the telephoto separates him from a blurred, rain-soft background. Rain streaks in the foreground become texture.
- **Light:** overcast monsoon, natural. Backlight the rain if any sun breaks through (rain only shows when backlit; place a backlight if needed).
- **Frame rate:** 96fps for boots hitting puddles and the salute.
- **Rain:** real monsoon preferred; rain towers as backup.
- **Grade:** cool, desaturated greens, deep blacks. One warm tone: the school flag.
- **Sound:** heavy rain, boots in water, his breathing, a distant "Left, right" in his own voice. Low string swell.
