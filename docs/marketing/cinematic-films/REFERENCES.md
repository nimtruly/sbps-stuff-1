# Cinematic references

Researched 16 Nov 2026 on Instagram keyword search ("school cinematic video", "cinematic robotics", "NDA cinematic", "cinematic ad film"). Kept reels with high views relative to followers, then downloaded each one, pulled 12 frames and transcribed the audio. Notes below describe what is actually on screen, not the caption.

Multiple = views ÷ followers. Above 100x means the video travelled on its own, not because of the account.
