## Logline
A mother sits in the stands at SBPS's morning drill, watching her son march. We never see him clearly until the last shot. The film is her face. It ends with a line that every Dehradun parent understands, because IMA is a few kilometres away.

## Why it can travel
The two highest-multiple NDA reels both put the camera with the people watching, not the cadets. Parents are the ones who choose a school, and this film is about them. It is also the most "ad film" of the five: premium, emotional, and specific to Dehradun.

## Look
- **Lens:** 85mm and 135mm at f/1.4-2 for her; the marching contingent is always a soft, rhythmic blur of uniform colours behind or in front of her.
- **Depth of field:** the whole film lives in the rack focus between her and him. He is out of focus until the last 5 seconds.
- **Foreground:** shoot through other spectators' shoulders, a striped pole, a flag (like A6) so she is in the middle layer.
- **Light:** winter morning sun, low and warm, mist in the background. Dehradun pines if visible.
- **Grade:** warm, golden, gentle contrast.
- **Sound:** commands, boots in rhythm, a band if the school has one. Her breath. No music until the end.
