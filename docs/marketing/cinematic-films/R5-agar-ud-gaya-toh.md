# R5 · "Agar Ud Gaya Toh?" (What If It Flies?)

**Topic:** Robotics · **Runtime:** 20s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps + 120fps
**Reference:** [A8 @neilchh Nike spec ad, 844K, 101x](REFERENCES.md) (ground-level golden hour, one line, one super)

## Logline
Golden hour on the school ground. A 12-year-old sets a student-built drone (or rover) on the grass, everyone holding their breath. One line of voiceover. It lifts off.

## Why it can travel
The reference was shot by students in a free period and still felt like Nike, because of three choices: lens on the ground, golden-hour backlight and a single line. Short, beautiful and rewatchable. It makes the robotics lab feel like sport.

## Look
- **Lens:** 24mm on the ground (lens almost touching the grass); 135mm telephoto for faces across the field.
- **Depth of field:** extreme. Grass blades in the foreground fully blurred, the machine sharp, the school building as soft shapes behind.
- **Light:** backlit golden hour, sun flare into the lens on purpose (anamorphic flare filter if available). Mountains of the Doon valley in the distance if the angle allows.
- **Frame rate:** 120fps for lift-off and the kid's reaction.
- **Sound:** wind, grass, a heartbeat-like low drum, then the rotor whine at lift-off.
