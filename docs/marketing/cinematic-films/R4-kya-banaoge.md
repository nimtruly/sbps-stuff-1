# R4 · "Kya Banaoge?" (What Will You Build?)

**Topic:** Robotics · **Runtime:** 40s · **Format:** 9:16, 4K, 24fps
**Reference:** [A10 Dior "Back to School", 1.93M](REFERENCES.md) (deadpan kid monologues to a wide lens)

## Logline
Six SBPS kids, aged 6 to 15, look straight into the lens and answer one question: "Agar kuch bhi bana sakte, toh kya banaoge?" Their honest, funny, serious answers cut against short shots of them actually building in the lab.

## Why it can travel
The Dior spot works on pure personality: kids being completely themselves at close range. Answers like "ek robot jo mera homework kare" are the hook, and the audience stays to hear the next kid. It also makes the school look premium.

## Look
- **Lens:** 24mm or 28mm at about 50 cm from the face. Slight wide-angle intimacy, eye-level, kid looks into the lens (use an Interrotron or a mirror box so they look at the director but into the lens).
- **Depth of field:** f/2. The lab or classroom behind each kid is soft colour, not detail.
- **Light:** soft window key, one warm practical in every background (desk lamp, LED strip). Each kid gets a different background colour block (school uniform colours: navy, red, gold).
- **Movement:** locked for answers. Whip pans for transitions between kids (pan blur into the next kid's whip).
- **Grade:** editorial, warm skin, slightly lifted blacks.
- **Sound:** clean lav or boom. Quiet piano or plucked score under; it stops for the funniest answer.

## Structure

| # | Time | Shot | Notes |

## Answers
**Do not script the answers.** Ask every kid the same question, roll for 10 minutes each, and use what they actually say. The lines above are only examples of the range to look for.
