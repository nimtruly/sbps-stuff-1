## Logline
A match-cut film that grows one boy up. A 6-year-old in a toy army cap salutes a passing army truck. Match cut: the same salute, now a Class IX boy on the SBPS ground. Match cut: the same salute, a Class XII student in front of the school flag. Three different students, one dream.

## Why it can travel
Match cuts are the most satisfying cinematic device for short video, because each cut is a small reward. Dehradun's army landscape (cantonment roads, pines, IMA nearby) makes it local. Parents of the 6-year-old and the 14-year-old both see their child in it.

## Look
- **Lens:** the same focal length (50mm) and the same camera height for every matching pair, so the cuts land precisely. Use a marked frame or onion-skin overlay on the monitor.
- **Depth of field:** f/2. The subject always sharp in the same spot of the frame; the background changes completely (home lane → school ground → flag).
- **Movement:** locked for the match moments; slow push-ins between them.
- **Light:** three times of day: dawn (age 6), morning (Class IX), golden hour (Class XII). The grade warms as he grows.
- **Sound:** one continuous music track. A natural sound accent on every match cut: truck horn, whistle, flag halyard.
