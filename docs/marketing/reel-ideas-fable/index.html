Before anything is shot

  • Consent. Written parental consent for every child on camera, and agreement from every teacher or parent who acts.
  • Facts are blanks. Class numbers, names, timings and kit details are left for the school to fill in. Nothing here claims a real achievement.
  • One programme claim. "NDA preparation from Class 9" is the only claim used, because the school's own documents support it.
  • Brand last. No logo or school name before the final three seconds. Admissions line and phone number go in the caption.
  • Music. Original or licensed tracks, so the same cut can run as a boosted post.
  • No guarantee. These copy the mechanism of reels that earned their reach. That raises the odds; it does not promise a hit.
