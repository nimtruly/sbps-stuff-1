## Risks and fixes

| # | Risk | Why it matters | Fix | In V2? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weak first 2 seconds (parent walking) | Hook fails, so the reel dies early | Open on sound: whistle and "SQUAD, FALL IN!", parent's head snaps round | Yes |
| 2 | Too much dialogue explaining features | Feels like an ad, people swipe | Student says 1–2 lines; features go on screen over B-roll | Yes |
| 3 | Fake academy posters with a made-up name | Could look like a jab at real coaching centres, or match a real name by accident | Generic "DEFENCE COACHING · ADMISSION OPEN" flyers, no name, no logo, no phone | Yes |
| 4 | Late student is a confusing role | Why is a late kid explaining the school? | Make it comedy: he answers while jogging backwards, then the coach calls and he sprints mid-sentence | Yes |
| 5 | Over 45s | Too many beats: walk, hear, look, talk, run, features | Cut the walk. Parent is already at the boundary | Yes |
| 6 | Parent actor looks staged | Indian audiences spot "ad acting" fast | Cast a real parent or staff member in everyday clothes | Yes (shoot notes) |

## Shoot notes

- **Parent:** cast a real parent or staff member in everyday clothes. No heavy makeup, no "ad smile".
- **Flyers:** design 2–3 generic ones in different colours. No academy name, logo or phone number, and nothing that resembles a real Dehradun academy.
- **Consent:** written parental consent for every student on camera.
- **Uniform:** PT kit and BDA bibs only. No army uniform or insignia on students.
- **Audio:** record the whistle and drill commands live. Keep one version on ad-safe licensed music for boosting.
- **Captions:** burn in Hinglish subtitles.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep text out of the bottom 20% and right edge.
