## How versions work

- V1 is the idea exactly as briefed, with no improvements. Later versions apply the fixes in the risk tables.
- Never edit an old version. Add a new one below it and log the change.

## Risks and fixes

### Idea 1: The fall (S1)

| # | Risk | Why it matters | Fix (for V2+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Looks like every army motivation edit | Viewers have seen "fall, get up, never give up" hundreds of times, so they scroll past | Add a twist. One cadet turns back and lifts him, which shows teamwork and the officer-like qualities SSB tests |
| 2 | Coach only says "get back to practice" | Can read as harsh or uncaring to parents | Coach watches, then nods or offers a hand. Firm but caring, like a mentor |
| 3 | No school identity until the B-roll | First 10s could be any academy anywhere | Show bib numbers and the BDA flag in frame from second 3 |
| 4 | B-roll is a feature list | Ad feel, retention drops | 1–1.5s per shot with 3–4 word text, cut on the beat |
| 5 | Fall stunt with a minor | Injury risk and parental complaints | Soft ground or mat, rehearsed controlled fall, parent consent form |
| 6 | No reason for a parent to act | Kids enjoy it, but parents are the buyers | End line aimed at parents: "School + NDA prep, ek hi campus" |

### Idea 2: The parent (S2)

| # | Risk | Why it matters | Fix (for V2+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weak first 2 seconds (parent walking) | Hook fails, so the reel dies early | Open on sound: whistle and "SQUAD, FALL IN!", parent's head snaps round |
| 2 | Too much dialogue explaining features | Feels like an ad, people swipe | Student says 1–2 lines only; features go in on-screen text over B-roll |
| 3 | Fake academy posters | Could look like a jab at real coaching centres or copy a real name | Generic "DEFENCE COACHING – ADMISSION OPEN" flyers, no name or logo |
| 4 | Late student is a confusing role | Why is a late kid explaining the school? | Make it comedy. He explains while jogging backwards, then the coach calls and he sprints mid-sentence |
| 5 | Over 45s | Too many beats: walk, hear, look, talk, run, features | Cut the walking. Start with the parent already at the boundary |
| 6 | Parent actor looks staged | Indian audiences spot "ad acting" fast | Cast a real parent or staff member, natural clothes, phone held in hand |

### Idea 3: Combined (S3)

| # | Risk | Why it matters | Fix (for V2+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two stories in under 45s | Viewer doesn't know whose story it is | Make the parent the eyes of the story; everything is seen from the parent's view |
| 2 | Two student characters (fallen and late) | Confusing and costs screen time | Merge them: the fallen student is the one who answers the parent |
| 3 | Runs long | Easy to go over 45s | Hard time budget: hook 3s, story 12s, B-roll 15s, payoff 5s, end card 3s |
| 4 | Tone clash (emotional fall vs comic late kid) | Mood whiplash | Keep the fall serious; the only light beat is "Yahi school hai, aunty!" |
| 5 | Same production risks as S1 and S2 | Stunt safety, fake posters | Apply S1 fix 5 and S2 fix 3 |

## Shoot notes (all scripts)

- **Uniform:** school PT kit and BDA bibs only. No army uniform or insignia on students; civilians wearing army uniform is an offence in India.
- **Fall:** mat or soft ground, rehearsed controlled fall, a stand-by first-aid kit and a teacher present.
- **Consent:** written parental consent for every student who is filmed.
- **Fake brochures:** design 2–3 generic ones ("DEFENCE COACHING · ADMISSION OPEN") with no academy name, no phone number and no logo resembling a real one.
- **Audio:** record clean drill commands live. Keep a version on licensed or Instagram ad-safe music for boosting.
- **Captions:** burn in Hinglish subtitles, because most viewers watch muted.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep text inside the safe zone, away from the bottom 20% and right edge.
