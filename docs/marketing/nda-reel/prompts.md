# NDA reel: storyboard and poster prompts (Google Flow)

These prompts are for generating storyboard frames in Google Flow. Each shot prompt (P01–P50) can be pasted on its own, because every prompt repeats its setting and characters.

- **Board IDs** match the **Board** column in `S1-the-fall.md`, `S2-the-parent.md` and `S3-combined.md`.
- **Reference frames** come from SBPS's earlier reels, in `E:\Projects\_Commercial\SBPS_DOON\reels-made\frames_sel\`. Each filename starts with the board ID it matches. Upload one as an image reference in Flow to match the look.
- **Look:** match the earlier SBPS reels. Teal-and-orange cinematic grade, golden hour or soft dawn light, shallow depth of field on close-ups.
- **On-screen text:** don't put reel captions into the generated frames; add them later in the edit. Posters (PO1–PO6) are the only prompts that ask for printed text. AI text often comes out garbled, so fix it in Canva afterwards.

## Characters (keep consistent)

| Name | Description used in prompts |
|---|---|
| Cadet A | Indian boy, about 15, short black hair, lean. White polo T-shirt with a navy collar, navy track pants with a red side stripe, teal mesh bib with a white number "08" |
| Teammate B | Indian boy, about 16, taller. Same uniform, teal bib number "12" |
| Junior | Indian boy, about 12. Same uniform, teal bib number "21" |
| Coach | Fit Indian man in his 50s, grey moustache, black polo shirt, black cap, whistle on a lanyard |
| Officer | Retired Indian colonel in his 60s, short grey hair, navy blazer and tie, at a wooden desk |
| Parent | Indian mother, early 40s, cotton salwar kameez with dupatta, shoulder bag |
| Squad | 8–10 Indian boys and girls aged 12–17 in the same uniform and teal numbered bibs |

## Location (keep consistent)

Dehradun school training ground: dusty brown and green field; white-painted steel obstacles (horizontal vault bars, an A-frame cargo net, low white balance beams, rope-climb poles); a teal shade-net fence; yellow school buses parked behind; a red-and-white four-storey school building; hazy Doon hills on the horizon.

## Camera key

| Code | Camera and lens | Look to ask for in the prompt |
|---|---|---|
| A7S3 | Sony A7S III + 16–35mm f/2.8 | "16mm ultra-wide, slight edge stretch" or "35mm, f/2.8, shallow depth of field", cinematic full-frame |
| ZV | Sony ZV-E10 + 16–50mm | "50mm tight framing, mild background blur" or "24mm natural perspective" |
| OA4 | DJI Osmo Action 4 | "action-camera ultra-wide, ground-level or chest-mount POV, slight fisheye" |
| 360 | Insta360 360° camera | "follow-cam from behind or beside a running subject, wide reframed 9:16" |
| DRONE | Drone | "aerial, top-down or high oblique, 9:16" |
