## Risks and fixes

| # | Risk | Why it matters | Fix | In V2? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Looks like every army motivation edit | Viewers have seen "fall, get up, never give up" hundreds of times | A teammate turns back and lifts him: teamwork, the quality SSB group tasks test | Yes |
| 2 | Coach only says "get back to practice" | Can read as harsh or uncaring to parents | Coach watches, lets the teammate help, then nods. Firm but caring | Yes |
| 3 | No school identity until the B-roll | First 10s could be any academy | BDA bib number and flag in frame from second 0–3 | Yes |
| 4 | B-roll is a feature list | Feels like an ad, retention drops | 1–1.5s per shot, 3–4 word text, cut on the beat | Yes |
| 5 | Fall stunt with a minor | Injury risk and parent complaints | Mat or soft ground, rehearsed controlled fall, consent form | Yes (shoot notes) |
| 6 | Nothing for the parent to act on | Kids enjoy it but parents are the buyers | End line for parents: "School + NDA prep. Ek hi campus." | Yes |

## Shoot notes

- **Fall:** mat or soft ground, rehearsed controlled fall, first-aid kit and a teacher on set.
- **Consent:** written parental consent for every student on camera.
- **Uniform:** PT kit and BDA bibs only. No army uniform or insignia on students.
- **Audio:** record coach lines live. Keep one version on ad-safe licensed music for boosting.
- **Captions:** burn in Hinglish subtitles.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep text out of the bottom 20% and right edge.
