## Shoot notes

- **Fall:** mat or soft ground, rehearsed controlled fall, first-aid kit and a teacher on set.
- **Parent:** a real parent or staff member in everyday clothes.
- **Posters and flyers:** V1 uses PO1–PO2 (made-up academy; check the name isn't real). V2+ use generic PO3–PO5.
- **Split screen (V4):** shoot both halves with a 9:16 frame and keep the subject in the centre third so each half crops cleanly to 1080×960.
- **360 transition (V5):** film a 360 spin in the interview room and on the ground with the same camera height; join them in post.
- **Interview panel:** the real ex-army staff, with their consent.
- **Consent:** written parental consent for every student on camera.
- **Uniform:** PT kit and BDA bibs only. No army uniform or insignia on students.
- **Captions:** burn in Hinglish subtitles.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep text out of the bottom 20% and right edge.
