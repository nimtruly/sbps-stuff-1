## Risks and fixes

| # | Risk | Why it matters | Fix | In V2? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two stories in under 45s | Viewer doesn't know whose story it is | Make the parent the eyes of the story; we see it from the parent's side | Yes |
| 2 | Two student characters (fallen and late) | Confusing and costs screen time | Merge them: the fallen student is the one who answers the parent | Yes |
| 3 | Runs long | Easy to go over 45s | Hard time budget: hook 3s, story 12s, B-roll 15s, payoff 5s, end card 4s | Yes |
| 4 | Tone clash: emotional fall vs comic late kid | Mood whiplash | Keep the fall serious; the only light beat is "Yahi school hai, aunty!" | Yes |
| 5 | Same production risks as S1 and S2 | Stunt safety, fake posters | S1 fixes 1, 3, 5 and 6; S2 fixes 1, 2, 3 and 6 | Yes |

## Shoot notes

- **Fall:** mat or soft ground, rehearsed controlled fall, first-aid kit and a teacher on set.
- **Parent:** cast a real parent or staff member in everyday clothes.
- **Flyers:** generic only. No academy name, logo or phone number.
- **Consent:** written parental consent for every student on camera.
- **Uniform:** PT kit and BDA bibs only. No army uniform or insignia on students.
- **Audio:** record coach lines and drill live. Keep one version on ad-safe licensed music for boosting.
- **Captions:** burn in Hinglish subtitles.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep text out of the bottom 20% and right edge.
