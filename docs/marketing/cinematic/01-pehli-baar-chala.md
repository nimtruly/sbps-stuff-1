# 01. Pehli Baar Chala (robotics)

**Logline:** The thirty seconds before a child's robot moves for the first time, told almost entirely in close-up.

**Length:** 30s (organic). **Format:** 9:16. **Language:** Hinglish, one spoken line.

**References:** [school nostalgia macro film](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C_hxpnny8YZ/) (49.5M) for the look; [robot arm build journey](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaaeW_DKnTF/) (7.85M) for the order of shots. See [00-references.md](00-references.md).

**Why it should travel:** the viewer is waiting for one thing, whether it moves. Every shot before that delays the answer.

## Look

- **Lenses:** 100mm macro for parts and eyes, 50mm and 85mm for faces. Nothing wider than 35mm until the last shot.
- **Aperture:** as open as the lens allows (f/1.8 to f/2.8). On macro shots only one screw or one LED should be sharp.
- **Light:** room lights off. One soft key from a window or a panel at the side, plus the robot's own LEDs and the laptop screen as practicals. Let the background fall to near black.
- **Frame rate:** 25 fps, with the final movement and the reaction at 50 or 100 fps for slow motion.
- **Grade:** warm skin, cool teal shadows, so the LEDs glow.
- **Movement:** slow slider pushes on objects. Handheld only for the reaction.

**Voice:** none except the child's own "Chal gaya" (or whatever they really say when it works).

## Edit notes

- Shots 1 to 7 get faster: 2s, 2s, 2s, 2s, then the pause.
- The two seconds of nothing in shot 8 are the film. Do not trim them.
- Cut the music at the switch and bring it back when the wheels move.
- Keep text out of the bottom 20% and top 10% of the frame.

## Versions

- **Organic, 30s:** as above.
- **Ad cut, 20s:** shots 1, 2, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, then an end card with the admissions line and number.
- **Loop cut, 9s:** shots 6 to 9 only.

## Needs from the school

1. A robot from the school's kits that reliably drives forward on a switch. Rehearse it; shoot the reaction on the first real take if you can.
2. The builder's class for the text card.
3. Parental consent for the three students.
4. A room that can be blacked out.

## Risk

If the reaction is staged it will look staged. Give the builder a robot they have not yet seen working, or film a real first run during a robotics period.
