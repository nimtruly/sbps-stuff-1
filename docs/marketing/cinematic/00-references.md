# Cinematic reels: references and findings

Research date: 16 Nov 2026. For [@sbpsdehradun](https://www.instagram.com/sbpsdehradun/). Topics: robotics and NDA.
This folder is separate from the earlier phone-shot scripts in `docs/marketing/reels-robotics-nda-outlier-scripts.md`, which are unchanged.

## How this was researched

- 22 more Instagram searches (school ad film, school cinematic, robotics cinematic, NDA cinematic, cadet and army cinematic, school promo, admission ad).
- About 650 reels catalogued, view counts pulled for about 250.
- 21 downloaded. I looked at an 8-frame sheet for 15 of them. Follower counts checked for 6 accounts.
- Not done: audio was not reliably transcribed (most are music or poetry beds), so notes on voice-over come from captions and on-screen text. Frame sheets show composition and grade, not camera movement or cut rhythm, so watch the links for those.

## The main finding

**Cinematic reels that travel are built on one feeling and a handful of detail shots. Brochure films do not travel.**

| | Example | Views | Shares | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Travels | School nostalgia film, @shotbypriyansh | 49.5M | - | Macro details: a book cover, an eye, chalk on a board, the bell, a pile of bags, shoes on brick. No presenter, no campus tour. |
| Travels | "First day / last day of school", @sciencemulmed | 22.1M | 183K | Two boys, the same frames repeated years apart. 22 seconds. |
| Does not travel | Presenter walks through the campus, 90s, @krmangalamgk2 | 2.0M | 9 | Polished, well lit, logo in the corner. 9 shares on 2M views suggests the views were paid for. |
| Not cinematic | "Admission open" with an AI poster, @ipischool14 | 21M | 3.3K | Phone clip of children on a lawn plus a generated poster. It got views, but it is not the look you asked for. |

So the brief for the crew is: **shoot it like a film, but about one small true moment**, not about the school.

## What the winners share

1. **A feeling in one line.** Nostalgia. Pride. "This took me months." Not "our facilities".
2. **Details before faces.** The first shot is an object or a hand, very close, with most of the frame out of focus.
3. **Sound carries half of it.** Chalk, a bell, boots, rain, a motor. Record it properly on the day.
4. **Short.** 15 to 25 seconds for nearly all of them. The two-minute films are the exception.
5. **Few words.** One line of text or a few lines of voice. None of them explain.
6. **No logo until the end**, if at all.

## Music and rights

Most of these use film songs or trending audio. That is fine for an organic post from Instagram's own library. A boosted post or ad cannot use most of that library, so each concept here is designed to work on recorded sound plus a licensed or original score. Cut the organic version and the ad version separately.
