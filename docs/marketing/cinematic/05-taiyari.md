# 05. Taiyari (NDA)

**Logline:** A student getting ready for parade, shown only through the objects: polish, brush, belt, lace, cap. The face comes last.

**Length:** 25s. **Format:** 9:16. **Language:** no speech; one line of text.

**References:** [school nostalgia macro film](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C_hxpnny8YZ/) (49.5M) for object-by-object storytelling; ["the final act"](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfCxdVbyVlY/) (1.97M) for silhouettes in haze at the end. See [00-references.md](00-references.md).

**Why it should travel:** it is the nostalgia film's method applied to discipline. Anyone who has served, been in NCC, or polished school shoes on a Sunday night recognises every object. That recognition is what gets shared.

## Look

- **Lens:** 100mm macro for nearly everything. One 85mm portrait at the end.
- **Aperture:** f/2.8 on macro, so only a sliver is sharp. Pull focus through each object slowly.
- **Light:** one window, early morning. Dust in the light if it is there naturally. A single hard backlight for the brush and polish so the bristles catch it.
- **Surface:** a wooden bench or a steel trunk. Texture matters.
- **Frame rate:** 50 fps, used at half speed for cloth and brush.
- **Grade:** warm and low-contrast, like old film. Deep blacks in the leather.
- **Movement:** slider only. Every shot moves a few centimetres.

## Edit notes

- Let each sound play clean. No music until shot 8.
- Two seconds an object. Do not rush the brush.
- The face is the reward. Nothing above the collar before shot 8.

## Versions

- **Organic, 25s:** as above.
- **Ad cut, 15s:** shots 2, 4, 6, 9, 10 and an admissions end card.
- **Sound-on cut:** the same picture with no music at all. The reference shows this style of clean recorded sound works; test it against the scored version.

## Needs from the school

1. What the NDA students actually wear and maintain: polished shoes, belt, cap or beret, badge. Shoot only the real items. If there is no cap, end shot 8 on the collar.
2. Real parade length for the text line.
3. A room with one good window, or a dormitory if there is one.
4. Consent for the lead and the students in the final line.

## Risk

Borrowed army kit on a school child would be dishonest and could draw criticism. Everything in frame has to be the student's own.
