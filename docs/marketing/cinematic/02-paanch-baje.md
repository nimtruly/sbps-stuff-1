# 02. Paanch Baje (NDA)

**Logline:** One student, the hour before sunrise, and a few lines in their own voice about why they get up.

**Length:** 30s. **Format:** 9:16. **Language:** Hinglish voice-over.

**References:** [cadet in the rain with a poem](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaFCy-ePeDp/) (12.5M, 3,947x followers) for stillness and words; ["the alarm shows two roads"](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dbawd19yOGH/) (2.58M) for the long-lens side track and reflective voice. See [00-references.md](00-references.md).

**Why it should travel:** the defence audience on Instagram shares mood pieces about discipline. Nearly all of them are shot at real academies with adults. A school student doing the same thing is the new part.

## Look

- **Time:** blue hour, starting about 40 minutes before sunrise. The whole shoot is a 50-minute window, so rehearse the day before.
- **Lenses:** 85mm and 135mm for compression on the ground; 35mm for the interior.
- **Aperture:** wide open. The student is sharp, the ground and the squad melt behind.
- **Light:** no added light outdoors. Indoors, one warm practical (a bedside lamp or a phone screen).
- **Frame rate:** 25 fps; the run and the boots at 50 fps.
- **Grade:** cold blue outdoors, one warm note indoors. Let it be dark; do not lift the shadows.
- **Atmosphere:** if there is morning mist, shoot into it. Do not use a smoke machine outdoors.

## Voice-over

Recorded by the student, in their own words, after a conversation with the director about why they chose this. Do not write it for them. A guide for length and tone only:

> "Alarm bajta hai toh do raaste hote hain. So jao, ya uth jao.
> Main roz uthta hoon.
> Kisi ne bola nahi hai.
> Bas ek din wardi pehenni hai."

Four short lines over shots 3 to 9. If the student's real words are different, use theirs.

**On-screen text:** `NDA ki taiyari. Class 9 se.` at 27s. That is the one programme claim, and it is the one the school's own documents support. Logo in the last second.

## Edit notes

- No music for the first six seconds. Sound only.
- One cut per line of voice.
- Hold shot 5 for a full three seconds. Stillness is what the reference does well.

## Versions

- **Organic, 30s:** as above.
- **Ad cut, 20s:** shots 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10 with the admissions line on an end card.
- **Still-frame cut, 15s:** shot 5 alone, held, with the four lines appearing as text. This is the closest to the 12.5M reference and costs nothing extra.

## Needs from the school

1. A student in the NDA programme who really does get up for morning training, and the real time it starts.
2. Whether there is a hostel. If not, shoot shots 1 to 4 at the student's home with the family's permission, or start the film at shot 4.
3. What the students actually wear for morning PT. Use that; do not put them in army-style uniform if they do not normally wear it.
4. Consent for every student in frame.
5. Permission to be on the ground before sunrise.

## Risk

Voice-over written by adults for a child sounds false immediately. If the student's own words are plain, keep them plain.
